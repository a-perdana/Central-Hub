What you do here

Read every annual principal appraisal in the network — for oversight, not editing.

1
Filter the list. Narrow by school, academic year, status, band, or recommendation. The table summarises composite (1–4) and predicate band (A–F) per principal.
2
Open an appraisal. Click any row to see the full F1–F5 + F_LEAD score breakdown plus the six narrative reflections (PNF1–PNF6). Useful for coaching prep, succession planning, and FR calibration.
3
Read-only. Editing happens in AH /principal-appraisal-entry by the assigned Foundation Rep. Submitted appraisals are immutable at rule level — this view is the network-wide window into a system the schools own.
