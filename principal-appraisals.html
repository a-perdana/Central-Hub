What you do here

Read every annual principal appraisal in the network — for oversight, not editing.

1
Filter the list. Narrow by school, academic year, status or band. The table shows the composite score (0–100) and the A–F band for each signed-off appraisal. A draft has no composite or band: none is issued until F5 is complete and two valid F2 visits exist.
2
Open an appraisal. Click any row to see the area scores (F1–F5, plus F_LEAD at Lead level) and the narrative fields (PNF1–PNF5). Item-level scores stay inside Eduversal — the Foundation Representative / General Manager receives only the summary: composite, band, recommendation and agreed goals.
3
Read-only. The primary appraiser — the Chief Academic Officer / designated Academic Director — writes the record in AH /principal-appraisal-entry. Signed-off appraisals are immutable at rule level; a genuine error is fixed by a new record or an Eduversal-managed correction, never a silent edit.

Appraisals close the year; coaching opens the next one.

Read with the principal's whole year in mind. Valid scheduled observation visits feed F2, and only the late-year / year-end 360° cycle feeds F3. Coaching never feeds the appraisal: neither session content nor how many sessions took place counts toward F5 or any score (Principal Appraisal: Handbook 26-27 §2, §10).
