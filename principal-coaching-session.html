About this page

One 60-minute coaching conversation, captured in 5 stages. Each stage has its own time-box and prompt set. Save as you go — the form auto-keeps your draft until you submit. After submit the record becomes immutable.

Use Stage 1 — Personal check-in to listen, not to log. Anything written there stays between you and the principal. Stages 2–5 (commitments, focus area, evidence, next steps) are kept in the coaching record for you, the principal and a central admin (record audit only).

Who can use this form. Only the principal's assigned coach or mentor with a Central Hub account: the Year-1 Principal Mentor recorded in Induction Admin, who must hold active Eduversal Mentor Certification (Induction Module: Principal 26-27 §2.2), or the Year-2+ coach assigned on the Coaching Hub. A Director role on its own gives no access. A mentor who is the Foundation Representative / General Manager of a different school works in Academic Hub and cannot log sessions here yet.

Rhythm. In Year 1 the Principal Mentor meets the principal weekly in Window 1, every two weeks in Windows 2–3 and monthly in Window 4 (Induction Module: Principal 26-27). From Year 2, coaching is monthly through both semesters (Coaching Cycle Framework).

Coaching is never appraisal. Neither what is discussed nor how many sessions take place feeds F5 or any appraisal score, and the appraiser does not see coaching records (Principal Appraisal: Handbook 26-27 §2, §10). If a topic belongs in the annual appraisal or a school visit, record it there instead.
