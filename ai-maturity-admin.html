AI Maturity Self-Assessments

Every Eduversal partner school self-assesses its AI integration once a year, across six areas. This page lists the profiles schools have shared. Open a school to read its six-area profile and add developmental support notes. The self-assessment is not validated or turned into a School Appraisal rating, and it keeps six separate area levels — no single overall score.

1
The school self-assesses Leadership team · AH
The leadership team, department heads and representative teachers rate the school across 6 areas, with evidence, strengths and improvement areas. The AI Integration Coordinator coordinates it; the Principal records the agreed ratings and marks the profile final.
2
You read the profile Eduversal Academic Directorate
Pick a school from "Shared — no notes yet" and read each area. Use it to see support needs, professional-learning priorities and network trends. You do not validate or change the school's ratings.
3
Add support notes Eduversal Academic Directorate
Write developmental notes and up to 8 concrete support actions, then save. The school sees them on its AH page as Eduversal notes, not as a rating.
i
Developmental, not an appraisal. School Appraisal does not score this profile. Where AI practice is relevant, appraisal teams look at the same evidence inside the five School Appraisal domains, using the Evidence Guide and Rating Rubric. Keep your notes specific and supportive — what Eduversal will help the school with — not a judgement of its ratings. Marked-final profiles are locked on the school side · six area levels, never one overall score · network reporting may summarise patterns but never ranks schools · notes and support actions become visible to the school after save.
