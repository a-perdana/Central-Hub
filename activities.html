About this page
An HQ kanban for projects and tasks — each card has a status (To-Do / Doing / Blocked / Done), an owner, and a due date. Action-tagged agenda items on Meetings spawn cards here automatically. Filter by "Owner = me" to see your own work; a "Doing" card stuck for 3 weeks is your reminder to either finish it or reset it.
