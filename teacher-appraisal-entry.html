About this page
An Eduversal Subject Specialist scores a lesson live during an on-site visit, using the F2 — Class Observation rubric. Items are filtered to the teacher's level (37 at Developing, up to 45 at Lead). You write it; the teacher reads it once you share. Anchored to ES 17.8.
This is Eduversal's own view of teaching quality — it is not part of the school's formal appraisal record and never contributes to a composite score or predicate band. The partner school runs its own F1–F4 cycle in Academic Hub.
