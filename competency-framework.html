Specialist Track — browse + self-assess

Six domains, hybrid by design: coaching-observer (cof / tpd) + subject-deepening (csm / cqa) + network-strategy (nls / xen). Tap any competency to open it and rate yourself on each level's statements.

  1. Self-assess here — rate yourself 1–4 on each statement. Scores stay private, feed the "Avg Self-Assessment" tile above
  2. Submit evidence in Specialist Portfolio when you're ready to claim a level officially
  3. HQ reviews in Competency Admin → approved level lands on your Learning Path + Certificates
⚠️ Self-assessment alone is not a certificate. It's an honest snapshot for your own reflection — earning a level requires evidence + HQ approval.

Primary international anchor for what a Cambridge teacher should know and do across planning, classroom, assessment, and professional growth.
