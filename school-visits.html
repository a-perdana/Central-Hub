What you do here

Plan visits, log outcomes, and track the appraisal cycle from one screen.

1
Log a visit. Use the Visits tab to record on-site monitoring, mid-year check-ins, validation visits, and follow-up notes. The phase strip shows where you are in the annual cycle.
2
Watch the network. The Network Appraisals tab rolls up teacher appraisals, school self-appraisals, and walkthroughs across all partner schools — finalisation rates, scores, and per-school progress.
3
Open or close cycles. Admins open appraisal periods in the Cycles tab. A cycle being open is what lets teachers and schools submit — close it when the window ends.
