What you do here

Read every leadership-observation visit on partner-school principals — valid scheduled visits feed F2.

1
Filter the list. Narrow by school, status (draft / submitted) or visit type. The pill row in each entry shows how many of the eight foci were rated Established, Developing or Not Observed.
2
Open an observation. Click any row to read the focus-by-focus ratings, the observer's evidence notes and the narrative fields: Headline Strength, Priority Development Area, Agreed Follow-up Actions and the Principal's Reflection.
3
Feedback first, then F2. Each visit is formative feedback in its own right, with no numeric visit score. The ratings of valid scheduled visits — Sept–Oct and Feb–Apr, with the principal told in advance — feed F2 of the annual appraisal (Established = 4, Developing = 2, Not Observed is excluded). Other visits are formative context only. Submitted records are immutable; drafts are edited by the original observer in AH.
