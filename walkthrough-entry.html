About this page
A walkthrough is a brief drop-in — typically 10–15 minutes — scored Evident / Developing / Not Observed across five foci, plus Glow, Grow and Go. You write it; the teacher and their subject leader read it. Anchored to ES 17.8.
Walkthroughs never feed the formal appraisal score — records carry contributes_to_formal_score: false. The formal lesson observation is F2, on the Class Observation Entry page.
