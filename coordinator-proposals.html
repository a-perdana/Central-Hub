About this page
Brainstorm and debate cross-network topics — KPI weighting, MAP Testing, Diagnostic Tests — before they become binding. Anyone can open a proposal and add comments; the comment thread stays searchable. The Director sets the phase and, when consensus is reached, promotes a proposal to the Decisions register so the decision keeps an audit trail. This replaces the running Google Doc.

🧭 How proposals work — phases & who can do what
Draft
The author is still writing — problem, options, recommendation. Not yet shown for discussion.
Who: the proposal's author drafts it. Any coordinator can open a new proposal.
→
Open for comment
Discussion is live. The room reads the proposal and adds comments. This is the brainstorming stage.
Who: the Director opens it for comment. All coordinators read & comment.
→
Decided
Consensus reached. Promoted to the Decisions Register (network-wide) with a back-link — the decision keeps its audit trail.
Who: only the Director promotes & approves a decision.
→
Parked
Shelved for now but kept on the record — revisit later. Not rejected, just not active.
Who: the Director parks it. Can be re-opened anytime.
