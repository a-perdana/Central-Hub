Academic Basis · Teacher Appraisal Framework v2.1

Why our teacher appraisal is structured this way

The Eduversal Teacher Appraisal Framework v2.1 is the Cambridge-co-authored, level-aware adaptation of the most validated summative teacher evaluation systems in the world. Four-framework composite (Admin · Observation · Work · Student Survey informative), 4-band scoring (Distinguished / Proficient / Basic / Unsatisfactory) and the Glow/Grow/Go feedback protocol all draw on Danielson, Marzano, Cambridge Teacher Standards and the formative-summative literature, while satisfying Indonesia's legal PKG / PMM teacher performance regulations.

🌍 Global
Danielson Framework for Teaching
FFT · 1996, revised 2013, 2022 · ASCD

Four domains, 22 components — Planning & Preparation · Classroom Environment · Instruction · Professional Responsibilities. Our F1 (Admin) + F2 (Observation) + F3 (Work) split mirrors the FfT collection-timing model directly.

🌍 Global
Marzano Focused Teacher Evaluation Model
Marzano Research, 2017

23 elements across Planning · Instruction · Conditions for Learning · Professional Responsibilities. Multi-measure, 4-band scoring + coaching-focused feedback — every scored item in v2.1 carries coaching_focus + feedback_prompts + follow_up_indicators on this model.

🎓 Cambridge
Cambridge Teacher Standards 2023
Cambridge International · 6 areas, 27 standards

Knowledge · Planning · Teaching · Assessment · Professional Learning · Contribution. Every F1/F2/F3 item carries cambridge_standard_refs[] (87/87 coverage) — click any CTS X.Y chip in the Entry page for verbatim source.

🌍 OECD
OECD TALIS — Appraisal Feedback Culture
Teaching and Learning International Survey · 2018 / 2024

International evidence on appraisal frequency, feedback quality, and what teachers say they need from formal evaluation. The Glow/Grow/Go protocol + the End-of-Year Conference timing are derived from TALIS-evidenced feedback discipline.

🇬🇧 UK
UK Teachers' Standards + Appraisal Regulations
DfE 2011/2021 · Education (School Teachers' Appraisal) Regulations 2012

8 standards + Part 2 conduct. The legal model for UK statutory teacher appraisal — annual cycle, observed lesson, written report, signposted CPD. Our annual rhythm + Distinguished/Proficient/Basic/Unsatisfactory band labels follow the UK appraisal regulations.

🇮🇩 ID
Penilaian Kinerja Guru + Pengelolaan Kinerja Guru
PKG · Permendiknas 35/2010 · PMM 2024 (Platform Merdeka Mengajar)

14 kompetensi (pedagogik · kepribadian · sosial · profesional) — legally referenced for guru ASN. Our F1/F2/F3 cross-walks these kompetensi with Cambridge standards so teachers don't double-enter data, and the v2.1 follow-up loop satisfies PMM's "Praktik · Observasi · Tindak Lanjut" cycle.

Why we keep formative and summative separate within the same instrument: Formative observation (walkthroughs · coaching cycles · peer feedback) shapes the teacher's next move; summative appraisal (this framework) records a verdict for the year. Combining them prematurely turns coaching into surveillance and demotivates the practice. Our v2.1 adds coaching_focus + feedback_prompts + follow_up_indicators to every scored item so the same instrument serves both — but the summative score is locked at the End-of-Year Conference and the formative loop continues separately. — Wiliam, Embedded Formative Assessment (2011); Killion, Assessing Impact (3rd ed., 2018); MET Project (Gates Foundation, 2013).
📚 Academic literature backing the v2.1 design
  • Danielson, C. (2007, 2013, 2022). Enhancing Professional Practice: A Framework for Teaching. ASCD — foundational rubric design + weighting logic + 4-band scoring.
  • Marzano, R. J., & Toth, M. D. (2013). Teacher Evaluation That Makes a Difference. ASCD — evidence base for multi-measure, weighted observation systems.
  • Wiliam, D. (2011). Embedded Formative Assessment. Solution Tree — the formative-summative distinction we operationalise via Glow/Grow/Go.
  • Killion, J. (2018). Assessing Impact: Evaluating Professional Learning (3rd ed.). Corwin — feedback-loop discipline + follow-up evaluation methodology.
  • Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation (2013). MET Project: Ensuring Fair and Reliable Measures of Effective Teaching. Empirical basis for combining observation + student perception + multi-source evidence in weighted composites.
  • Darling-Hammond, L. (2013). Getting Teacher Evaluation Right. Teachers College Press — weighting validity + growth-vs-accountability balance.
  • OECD (2024). TALIS 2018 / 2024 Results, Vol. I–II. International evidence on teacher appraisal practices and feedback quality.
  • EEF (2024). Effective Professional Development Guidance Report. Education Endowment Foundation — research-backed PD design discipline informing follow-up indicators.
🏷️ How framework tags work in this appraisal

The Teacher Appraisal Entry page (Academic Hub) renders CTS chips on every scored item so each F1/F2/F3 criterion can be traced to the Cambridge Teacher Standard it anchors. Click any coloured chip to open the verbatim source via the cross-reference popover:

  • CTS Cambridge Teacher Standards 2023 — 27 standards × 6 areas (87/87 v2.1 item coverage)
  • PKG Penilaian Kinerja Guru (Permendiknas 35/2010) — Indonesia legal 14-kompetensi anchor
  • PIGP Program Induksi Guru Pemula (Permendiknas 27/2010) — Year-1 teacher induction anchor
  • SKL Standar Kompetensi Lulusan (Permendikdasmen 10/2025) — Indonesia student-outcome standards
  • ES Eduversal Academic Standards — network-wide policy (madde-level, e.g. ES 7.3 = Teacher Appraisal System)
  • AICF AI Competency Framework v1.0 — AI literacy + teaching with AI
  • EEF EEF Effective Professional Development 2021 / 2024 — research-backed PD design discipline

Reviewers open chips on the Teacher Appraisal Entry page (AH) to verify a scored item's framework anchor. This CH page is the network-wide review surface — full per-item chip inspection lives in the entry tool where the appraiser worked.
