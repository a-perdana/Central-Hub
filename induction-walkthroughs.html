My Induction Walkthroughs

Your own induction visit record — the shadow, co-led and solo visits you log through the five phases of Subject Specialist Induction. Detailed mentor-review content stays between you and your Mentor Specialist; the Academic Director sees your phase, visit counts and sign-offs, not the review text (Induction Module: Specialists 26-27, §8). To leave feedback for a teacher, use Classroom Walkthrough instead. Aggregate visit view: /school-visits.

What you do here

Log your induction visits, phase by phase.

1
See where you are. Five phases over 12 months: Orientation & Cambridge Grounding, Shadow Visits, Co-Delivery, Supervised Independence, then Full Independence & Certification. Click a phase below to read what it asks of you.
2
Log each visit. Pick the phase and the visit type, then add your notes. In Phase 2 these are your own observer notes — you do not give feedback to the school. A child-protection concern always goes to the Designated Safeguarding Lead at once.
3
Submit for mentor review. Your Mentor Specialist reads it in Walkthrough Review and replies to you there. The review stays between the two of you. Full guide →
